# Agent notes

The glossary for this repository is [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md). The ingestion flow is [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).

Read CONTEXT.md before naming a domain concept. Use its term for that concept. Do not substitute a word listed under `_Avoid_`. When the concept is missing from CONTEXT.md, say so. Do not invent a synonym.

Read ARCHITECTURE.md before describing how a pool moves through discovery, the detail read, the tick book, and fit. The joined inputs a local quote reads are the quote inputs.

The language covers pool identity, pool shape, quote inputs, price curves, chain history, peers, and the services that own them.
